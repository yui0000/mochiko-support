Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Information We Collect

もちこ (Mochiko) does not collect any personal information. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no data is transmitted to any external server. No account or sign-in is required.

Data Stored in the App

The look of your creature (name, color, and the parts you chose), the items you got from the gacha, your heart gauge, your best scores in the mini games, and whether you have seen the first-time guide are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded or shared with third parties, and are removed when you delete the app.

Camera, Photos, Microphone, and Location

The app does not use the camera, the photo library, the microphone, or location information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies to any purchase transactions. Whether you have made a purchase is checked through StoreKit and is not stored by the app.
